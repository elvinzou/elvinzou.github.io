Privacy policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026. Contact: zoutao0012@gmail.com.

Data stored in Chrome

UpYouGo stores your language, reminder settings, timer state, cat appearance and position, excluded website hostnames, and the last seven local calendar days of confirmed activity statistics in Chrome local storage. The beta does not send these data to our server or synchronize them between devices. Uninstalling the extension removes its local storage; it does not delete files you exported yourself.

Website access

Optional HTTP/HTTPS website permission lets the extension draw its cat on pages you allow. It uses the active tab and hostname to decide whether to appear and to respect your exclusions. It does not read page text, form entries or browsing history, or store a list of sites you visited. Only the hostnames you choose to exclude are saved.

Other permissions

Storage retains settings and timer state. Alarms wake due reminders while Chrome runs. Idle detects when the computer is locked so unobserved time is not counted. Scripting adds the companion to authorized sites. Optional notifications show the same reminder through Chrome and your operating system. You may revoke optional access in Chrome or the extension’s settings.

Statistics and export

Today’s brief summary remains free. Seven-day charts and Excel/CSV export are temporarily previewable during the beta and are planned for the one-time upgrade. Exported files contain activity records and explanations, not settings or excluded websites. You can clear local statistics at any time, including without an upgrade. We will notify testers in advance and leave time to export before ending this preview.

Accounts, payments and contact

The current beta has no account, checkout, third-party analytics, advertising or automatic charge. A future paid launch will require an external payment provider and purchase verification; this policy will be updated before any payment data is collected. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. Request deletion of support correspondence by replying to the same address. Do not send sensitive page contents, bank details or passwords.
