How it works

A small pause, when you need one.

1. Set your pace

Pick short breaks or sit/stand, then choose the durations and work hours that suit you.

2. Let the cat join

Website and system-notification permissions are optional. The toolbar timer works even if you decline website access.

3. Confirm your change

UpYouGo waits for you to start a break, resume work or change posture. Paused and unobserved time is excluded from activity totals.
