Refund policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026. The invitation beta is free and accepts no payments, so there is currently nothing to refund.

When paid sales begin

You may request a full refund within 7 calendar days of the original one-time purchase. Email zoutao0012@gmail.com from the purchase email address with your order or receipt reference. Do not send card or bank details by email. We will route the request through the authorized payment provider; the provider’s receipt and refund process will identify the final transaction.

After a refund or chargeback is confirmed, premium access will be revoked. Free reminders and comfort controls remain usable, and the extension will not delete your local statistics or saved settings. Mandatory rights under applicable law remain unaffected.

Need help first?

If purchase recovery, an animation, or export fails, email the same support address. We can help investigate without asking you to send your browsing history or local activity report. Purchase and refund handling will be activated only after the merchant account and checkout are approved.
